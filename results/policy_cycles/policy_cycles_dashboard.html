What this does not show

  • Six complete cycles is a small number of independent observations. The 22 intervals above are descriptive, and I have not seen a multiple-testing correction applied across them.
  • Returns come from model-estimated par bonds (2-year and 8-year), not a tradable index. Roll-down, instrument choice, financing frictions and market impact are left out.
  • Event timing is partly reconstructed. 152 events are date-only and 30 scheduled holds are reconstructed, so each becomes usable only from the next calendar day.
  • The 2015-onward cycles were reserved but are not out-of-sample: their outcomes were seen in earlier work.
  • The rule was fixed in advance and not tuned, but one rule and one sample path make this a backtest, not evidence of a tradable edge.
