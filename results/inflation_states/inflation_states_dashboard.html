What this does not show

  • The economic hypotheses are inconclusive, not refuted. With about 20 independent inflation episodes the data cannot tell a modest effect from none.
  • No trading signal, cost, or Sharpe analysis was run, by design. Nothing here says a state is tradable.
  • CPI is not the Fed's PCE target measure, and inflation expectations and surprises were not used as state inputs.
  • Fed funds availability before 1996 and the 08:30 ET CPI release time are assumptions.
  • The reserved window from 2015 was visible when the rules were written, so it is not a prospective test. Only decisions after the freeze date are.
  • The October 2025 CPI release did not happen (federal shutdown), so momentum rules needing that month are undefined for the affected decisions.
